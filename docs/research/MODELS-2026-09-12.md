# Open-weight model landscape, 2026-08-20 to 2026-09-12

Scope: what changed in roughly three weeks, judged against the current 128 GB M5 Max setup running Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-oQ4e-mtp on oMLX 0.7.0.dev2, and against the 256 GB M5 Ultra arriving in November. Vendor numbers are flagged. Everything else is a third-party page with a date.

## Notes on the three-week haul

GLM-5.3-Flash is the only release that plausibly beats Flash-Next on agentic coding at a size a Mac can hold. Its DeepSWE v1.1 edge is 63.4 to 58.7, and its Terminal-Bench 2.1 figure of 84.3 is vendor-published with no independent replication I could find. The licence is the open question: TechNode and one aggregator call it MIT, innfactory says a bespoke GLM-5.3 licence. Check the repo before committing.

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash is the headline release but it moved further out of reach, not closer. The MLX conversions count 754.6B parameters once the Engram tables are included, and even the 50% REAP-pruned build wants ~320 GB with a measured 1.17x perplexity penalty against unpruned. It also needs the PipeNetwork custom runtime rather than stock mlx-lm. Hy4 is a datacentre model that happens to have open weights.

## Flash-Next itself

No oQ5e or oQ6e build exists yet. The oQ family is oMLX's importance-matrix quantizer, base 4-bit affine at group size 64 with sensitive tensors promoted to 5, 6 or 8 bits, and 4-bit is the only tier published for this model. The plain 6-bit conversion measures wikitext-2 perplexity 4.4767, +0.0013 against bf16, and the converter states 6-bit and 8-bit are statistically indistinguishable from bf16 on that corpus, while quantizing everything to 4-bit costs +20.6% against keeping non-expert weights at 8-bit. That last figure is the argument for a mixed 4/8 build if 128 GB can hold one.

Two known defects. Flash-Next enters a deterministic token-ID-0 repetition loop when thinking mode, at least one tool, and SGLang's qwen3_coder parser are all active (SGLang issue 36537). That is an SGLang path, not oMLX, but the trigger is thinking plus tools, which is exactly the agentic pattern. Separately, the vLLM recipe reports MTP speculative decoding making throughput worse at every concurrency tested on their hardware, so measure before trusting the MTP head.

The mlx-community 4-bit card documents a real bug worth knowing: earlier conversions applied the PLE offset twice and degraded generation, and group size 32 is required so the n-gram embedding dimensions quantize cleanly. Any older local conversion should be checked against that.

Community M5 Max 128 GB numbers: 33 tok/s on fresh context and 11 tok/s at 262K, with a one-shot 262K fill taking about 28 minutes. The 62 tok/s measured locally is well ahead of that, which is consistent with oQ4e plus the oMLX 0.7 work rather than a stock 4-bit conversion.

## oMLX and MLX

oMLX moved fast in the window. 0.6.3 (Aug 27) added first-class Flash-Next and GLM-5.3-Flash support. 0.6.4 (Aug 29) added exact QSA prefill and decode acceleration. 0.7.0.dev1 (Sep 10) claims 44 to 63% Flash-Next generation speedups across contexts plus a rebuilt distributed serving layer, Cluster v2. 0.7.0.dev2 (Sep 11) added DeepSeek V4.1-Flash with DSpark MTP and Engram SSD offload, INT8-activation prefill for Qwen on M5 Macs worth a claimed +34.4% prefill, experimental MoE expert SSD offload for DeepSeek, Qwen, Gemma 4 and OLMoE, and Lightning MTP for GLM-5.3-Flash.

Two of those matter here. INT8-activation prefill is M5-specific and free upside on the existing model, so it is worth confirming it is enabled. Experimental expert SSD offload is the thing that could eventually put a 200 GB-class model on 128 GB, but no throughput numbers are published for it yet, and the comparable third-party effort (flash-moe, ssd-moe/deepseek-v4-flash-mlx) reports 4.5 to 5 tok/s, which is unusable for agentic coding. Cluster v2 becomes interesting in November: an M5 Max plus an M5 Ultra could in principle share a GLM-5.3-Flash 6-bit build.

On low-bit quants, there is no validated 2-bit or ternary result for any model in this class. A 2-bit DeepSeek V4.1-Flash MLX build and a Ternary-Bonsai-27B exist, neither with published KL or task numbers. Treat both as experiments.

## Verdict

Nothing worth downloading to 128 GB. Flash-Next oQ4e remains the only model of its class that fits with real context, GLM-5.3-Flash cannot be squeezed below 177 GB, and DeepSeek V4.1-Flash moved further away rather than closer. The productive work on 128 GB this month is runtime-side: pick up 0.7.0.dev2, confirm INT8-activation prefill is on, and avoid the thinking-plus-tools parser path.

The November Ultra pick is unchanged. GLM-5.3-Flash 4-bit at 177.5 GB is still the target, now with a genuine second choice: Flash-Next 6-bit at 148 GB is near-lossless by measured perplexity and keeps the harness you already tuned. Run both for a week and pick on your own harness rather than on a vendor Terminal-Bench figure. Resolve the GLM licence question first. If the 512 GB Ultra is still on the table, that single decision unlocks DeepSeek V4.1-Flash at 427.6 GB, and it is the only reason to consider that config.
